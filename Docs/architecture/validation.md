Scene-library hierarchy coverage requires the single strict `Scenes.v25.json` contract, unique Production/Episode/Shot identities, exactly one placement for every scene, explicit unclassified membership, unique immutable scene ordinals per Shot and a next ordinal strictly greater than every allocated value. Movement between Shots is atomic, never mutates a scene snapshot and never reuses the source ordinal; movement within one Shot is identity. Entering a Shot adopts `<shot name>_<ordinal>` and prevents direct Scene renaming until unclassified. Episode association adopts the external slug and prevents direct renaming until free. Shot association adopts the external canonical name, prevents direct Shot renaming until free and refreshes every placed Scene name without changing its ordinal. Renaming a free Shot refreshes those derived Scene names. Presentation sorts scenes by stored name with UUID tie break rather than by ordinal directly. The selected v23-to-v24 maintenance migration preserves every scene record and order, verifies a byte-identical timestamped backup and places every scene in unclassified; startup rejects v23 without migrating it. The separately selected v24 associated-name maintenance normalization creates the same byte-identical backup, changes only associated Episode/Shot names and placed Scene names, and preserves every identity, authored snapshot, collection membership, ordering, state, reference and ordinal.
